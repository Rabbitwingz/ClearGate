How it works

Nothing about answering the gate changes. ClearGate just handles what comes after.

A visitor arrives

MyGate shows the entry request or delivery pre-approval, just like always.

You approve or deny

Your answer goes to the gate as usual. ClearGate never taps anything for you.

The ad is skipped

As soon as MyGate confirms your answer, ClearGate sends it to the background. No ad, no MyGate screen left open.

Small app, thoughtful details

Built to stay out of your way, including when you need to use your banking apps.

Guests and deliveries

Works for visitor entries and for delivery pre-approvals from apps like Swiggy, Zomato and Instamart.

Plays nicely with banking apps

Some banking apps refuse to open while any accessibility app is on. Open them from ClearGate's widget and it pauses itself first.

Widget, tile and shortcuts

A home-screen widget with your bank apps, a Quick Settings tile, and long-press shortcuts. Pause and resume however you like.

Never forget to resume

While paused, a notification keeps a Resume button handy, with a gentle nudge after 10 minutes.

Private by design

ClearGate uses Android's accessibility feature for one narrow job: noticing MyGate's post-answer screen and pressing Home. That's it.

  • Only sees the MyGate app. Every other app is invisible to it.
  • No internet permission. It can't send anything, anywhere.
  • No accounts, no tracking, no ads of its own.
  • Open source. Every line is on GitHub for anyone to read.

Questions

The accessibility switch is greyed out

Android 13 and newer restrict accessibility for apps installed outside the Play Store. Open Settings › Apps › ClearGate, tap ⋮ (top right) › Allow restricted settings, then turn it on again.

My banking app says an "unauthorized app" is active

Many banking apps block any accessibility app as an anti-fraud measure. ClearGate doesn't try to hide from them. Instead, open your banks from ClearGate's widget or long-press shortcuts: it pauses itself first, then opens the bank. Tap Resume when you're done.

Does ClearGate approve visitors for me?

No. You always answer the request yourself. ClearGate only acts after MyGate confirms your answer.

Why isn't it on the Play Store?

Play Store policy doesn't allow apps that interfere with another app's ads. ClearGate is a personal, open-source tool, so it's distributed directly from GitHub.

It stopped working after a while

Some phones aggressively stop background apps. Set ClearGate's battery usage to Unrestricted and check it's still on in Accessibility settings.
